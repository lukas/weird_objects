## Weakest 3 parts (highest peak stress)

1. **`coxa_link`** (case: `coxa_link_yaw_stall`): peak stress = 0.92 MPa, SF = 24.70.  comfortable margin.  _Likely critical region:_ bridge slab tying the horn yoke to the hip-pitch servo well (link x in [0, 25] mm).  Comment block at hexapod_prototype.py:~1610 documents repeated user complaints about this exact failure mode: the bridge-to-well bond is the narrow 'fuse' between the yaw output and the hip-pitch motor housing.
