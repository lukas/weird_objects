Quad mode — tip back, walk on four legs

The robot rears up nose-high like a begging dog — front legs tucked in the air — and moves on the four rear legs. Two gaits, both one scripted 20 Hz pose stream of rear up → go → sit back down, both with a LIVE speed slider: the walk is a lateral-sequence animal walk (footfalls LH·LF·RH·RF, one foot up at a time, statically stable, weight sway against the swing); the trot moves DIAGONAL leg pairs together like a horse — high-stepping front legs (45 mm knee action), ~3× the walk's pace, with a visible horse-prance (~10° tilt band, sim). Only two feet hold the floor mid-beat, so treat it as the more advanced act on hardware. Level-body 4-leg walking is geometrically impossible on this robot (CoM lands ahead of the 4-foot polygon) — rearing back is what makes it work. Sim-proven on the MuJoCo twin: walk margin never below +34 mm, peak ~2–2.6 A, no falls anywhere in the swept envelope (including 2×).
Floor: flat + non-slip, ~0.4 m clear ahead, no cords under the body. Watch it the whole run — first hardware runs at 0.5–0.75×, walk before trot. Stop / E-STOP abort instantly.
