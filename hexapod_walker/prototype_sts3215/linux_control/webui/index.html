Quad mode — tip back, walk on four legs

Split quad workflow: Rear up tips back and holds with the front legs tucked; Walk and Trot then run forward or backward while reared; Come down untucks the fronts and returns to stand. Walk is the steadier one-foot sequence; trot uses diagonal pairs and is experimental on hardware. Use the stance menu as a test ladder: Safe is the control, Cool is the candidate, then pitch/aft/high/step isolate one change at a time. Hold/walk/trot use live IMU pitch trim to nudge the body fore/aft and slow the gait if pitch drifts. Use a flat non-slip floor, clear slack around the body, and watch the robot the whole time.

Rocking chair

Sitting show — the chassis never stands, so it is safe on a desk. The mid pair (the two legs pointing straight out to the sides) stretches flat as outriggers, the front and rear pairs raise overhead, then the pairs press the floor in anti-phase — one comes down as the other goes up — and the body seesaws over the belly, about ±10° of pitch. Verified on the MuJoCo twin at 0.75×–1.5× tempo; the slider is clamped to that range. Homes to sit zero (legs out) before it starts.

Walk distance (tape)

Settles the sim's slip / contact pricing — the top calibration gap. Lay a tape measure along the travel line, mark the start foot position. Needs ARM + stand (P) on the Drive tab first. Scripted tripod gait; stops itself if tilt exceeds 30°.

Turn sign check

Settles the TURN sign audit: does commanded +ω really turn the body clockwise? One 6 s turn-in-place each way, then report which way it actually rotated (and the heading change if you can read it off the tape/floor marks). Needs ARM + stand (P).

Holding currents

Settles the last effort-pricing gap: how much current does HOLDING position cost, loaded vs unloaded? Run planted with the robot standing on its feet, then prop the body up so the feet hang free and run hover in the same pose. No commanded motion — it torques on and holds the present pose. Saves automatically.
